Background

During work experience on the strategy team at Laing O'Rourke, I attended a briefing where a finance lead demonstrated how a £100m contract with a 5% profit margin could be almost entirely wiped out by a 4% cost overrun. This tool was built to model that dynamic, showing how thin construction margins are and how quickly cost overruns become catastrophic.
